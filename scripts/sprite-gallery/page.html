Known issues

  • Attack and Skill are each hero's own weapon move. They are redrawn per frame, so a weapon can wobble slightly between frames.
  • Dash and Hurt still come from body templates; a few heroes lower or hide their weapon during those.
  • The game only plays east and west today; north and south are ready for a future top-down use.
  • The knight's side views read paler than its front.
  • Fixed 2026-10-11: the 4 flagged icons are replaced (oak leaf, blank price tag, plain chain, boxing glove); panel, card and health bar re-made without drips, skull or barcode; both buttons are one 150×68 box.
  • Fixed 2026-10-11: every hero Idle re-made holding its weapon (the old template idles made hair, capes and weapons blink in and out); leftover grey background patches cut out of the Oni Warlord's Walk, Run and Idle.
  • The two buttons still carry red paint drips.
  • Towers have Idle / Attack / Skill in east and west only (all the game reads for towers).
  • Oni Warlord needs a 264px canvas: its flaming katana and cape fill the whole frame. Its east Death ends kneeling; south ends flat.
  • Titan-X swaps which arm holds the cannon between some directions.

How to update this page

This page does not refresh by itself. After any sprite change, open Claude Code in the ATO repo (any chat) and say:

update the sprite gallery
  • Claude runs npx tsx scripts/sprite-gallery/build.ts, then republishes to this same link. Full steps: scripts/sprite-gallery/README.md.
  • Where everything lives: art in assets/play/skins/streetwear/; every PixelLab id, cost and weapon move in its MANIFEST.md; working log and raw exports in games/grove/ref/streetwear-source/ (STATE.md).
  • Sprite rules: Walk/Dash/Hurt use PixelLab skeleton-v3; Attack/Skill are custom v3 weapon moves; every frame is padded to one canvas per character (heroes 192px, enemies 96px, towers 236-244px like the cast, Oni Warlord 264px, Titan-X 244px) with feet aligned; 4 facings per clip.
  • Link to keep: https://claude.ai/artifact/9wjcLv4FQu4S8dwqT2RBho
