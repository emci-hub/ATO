Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE — fill in on publish] · Draft, pending lawyer review

ATO ("the app," "we," "us") is made by AsTrollOGs. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

This is not a substitute for legal advice. This draft is intended for review by a lawyer before ATO is publicly available.

What we collect

Account & profile — name, @handle, timezone (read from your device, not asked), onboarding answers (what you're in this week, what usually knocks you off, your morning anchor, your talk style), your color and pixel appearance.

Usage — daily Checks (did/skip), facts you've explicitly asked Sage to remember, messages you send in Chat with people in your Circle, referral relationships (used only to prevent abuse, never shown publicly).

Device — push notification token if enabled, crash and error reports (flag + timestamp only for safety-related flags — see Crisis section).

What we never collect or store: guesses about your mood or personality you didn't confirm, raw health data from your device, a model's freeform narrative written about you, your exact location.

Who else sees your data

We use a small number of service providers to run ATO. We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication. Your profile, Checks, chat messages, and facts live here.
  • AI providers (Gemini, NVIDIA, Perplexity, Claude, Grok) — power Sage, the in-app AI coach. Relevant context, not your full chat history, is sent to the configured provider to generate a response. Claude and Grok are called through our server so their keys never sit on the phone.
  • Resend — sends one-time login codes to your email.
  • Apple — if you sign in with Apple, Apple may relay a private email address instead of your real one.

None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes beyond providing the service to us.

Sage is a coach, not a person

Sage is an AI feature, not a human, and not a licensed therapist or counselor. Sage cannot provide medical, psychological, or emergency care, and should not be relied on as a substitute for either.

Crisis support

If a message contains language associated with a safety crisis, ATO shows a static support card with a crisis line for your region instead of sending your message to Sage. That a message was flagged, and when, is logged to improve detection — the message content itself is not stored for this purpose.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line directly — ATO is not equipped to respond to emergencies.

Chat is not end-to-end encrypted

Messages in Chat are encrypted in transit (TLS) and protected by access controls in our database, but are not end-to-end encrypted. It is technically possible for us to access message content if required (for example, to investigate a report of abuse). We don't read your messages otherwise.

Referral tracking

If you were invited to ATO, we keep a record of who invited you, used only to prevent abuse. Never shown publicly beyond your own choice to disclose who you've invited.

Age requirement

ATO is intended for people 16 and older. Marking yourself "going" to an 18+ night requires you to be 18 or older. These are self-reported.

Your controls

Visibility settings, block/mute/report on any Chat or Sage exchange, and in-app account deletion (permanent, revokes your Sign in with Apple token).

Changes to this policy

Material changes are flagged in-app before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@asstrollogs.com
