What we collect

Account & profile — your email address (to sign in) or the address Apple gives us, name, @handle, timezone (read from your device, not asked), city for Around (you pick it; we do not use GPS), date of birth (self-reported; we store the date so we can confirm you are 18 or older), your sign-up answers and your answers to ATO's questions (which build your trait profile), your color and pixel appearance.

Usage — daily Checks (the day's read and do text, kept for 7 days, and whether you did or skipped it, kept); your saved profiles and history; your Circle (friend requests, friends, blocks, the reports you file, and your per-friend switches for visits and compare); your Divecore game save (your pet, rooms, items and progress; kept if you reset your profile, deleted when you delete your account); anonymous daily counts of Divecore actions (no account or device attached); an AI usage log (which AI feature was used on which day and how many times, plus your ATO token activity; no request or reply text); referral relationships if invite or referral links are in use (used only to prevent abuse, never shown publicly). [CONFIRM: email form on the public landing page — it currently stores an email to request an invite; keep, change or remove to match launch.]

Device — crash and error reports through Sentry, sent with no name, email, handle or account ID and with free text stripped, so a report is not tied to your account [CONFIRM: Sentry region and retention]. Notifications are scheduled on your phone; ATO does not upload a push token to its servers.

What we never collect or store: guesses about your mood or personality you didn't give us, health data from your device, your exact location, your contacts, or advertising identifiers. ATO has no ads and does no tracking across other apps or websites.

What we do not do with what you type: we do not scan, flag or log it for safety or crisis detection, and nothing you type is sent to an AI model.

Who can see what

  • Anyone with your @handle (for example, from a card you share) can see your name, @handle, two of your sign-up answers (how you show up, how you talk) and your pixel appearance. Nothing else.
  • Friends in your Circle. Someone becomes a friend only when you accept their request (or they accept yours). A friend can visit your Divecore Hideout and send you preset gifts only while visits are switched on for that friend, and can see "Compare strengths" only if you both switch it on for each other: a few of your clear strengths as fixed phrases. Never your scores, your answers, your attachment or conflict style, the lower side of any trait, your Checks, or anything you typed. Each switch is per friend, compare is off by default, and you can turn either off at any time.
  • Nobody sees your answers, your trait scores, your Checks or your saved profiles.

Circle: blocking and reporting

Block anyone in your Circle or anyone who sent you a request from the ⋯ menu. A block is silent: the other person is not told. It removes the friendship and pending requests, ends visits and compare between you, and stops them finding you by handle in the app or sending a new request. (Your public card, described above, can still be opened by anyone who has your @handle.)

Report someone from the same menu by picking one of six fixed reasons. We keep the report (the reason, when it was filed and the reported @handle) even if either account is later deleted, so we can act on abuse; the reporter's identity is removed if the reporter deletes their account.

Names and handles are checked at sign-up and when changed: slurs and names that pretend to be ATO staff are refused. ATO has no direct messages between people today. [CONFIRM: if chat is added later, this section must be updated before it ships.]

Who else processes your data

We use a small number of service providers to run ATO. We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your profile, answers, Checks, Circle data and Divecore save live here.
  • AI model providers: Google (Gemini API), with DeepSeek as a fallback — ATO uses AI in two places only: Legends cards and category deep dives. Everything else, including Insights and the Story, is written ahead of time and picked on your phone; no model is called for it. The request goes from our server to Google's Gemini API, and if Gemini fails our server may send the same request to DeepSeek. Your phone holds no provider key and sends only the kind of card and a trait group (a short key worked out from your leanings), never text you typed. The prompt contains no name, user ID or answers. Cards are stored in a shared library and shown to other people with similar leanings. ATO logs which feature was called and when, not the prompt or the reply. [CONFIRM: no-training and retention terms for both providers, and DeepSeek (based in China) as a processor.]
  • Resend — sends one-time sign-in codes to your email. [CONFIRM: other email use and retention.]
  • Sentry — receives crash and error reports, not tied to your account.
  • Apple — if you sign in with Apple, Apple may relay a private email address instead of your real one. We store a token from Apple so we can revoke it when you delete your account.

[CONFIRM: that each provider's contract bars it from using your data for its own purposes; this cannot be verified from the app's code.]

Help if you're struggling

ATO is not a counselling or emergency service. On the You tab there is a fixed list of support lines: 988 for the US and Canada, findahelpline.com elsewhere, and your local emergency number. It is the same for everyone, it is not triggered by anything you type, and nothing about it is logged.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line directly.

Age requirement

ATO is for people 18 and older. Marking yourself "going" to an 18+ night also requires a valid birthday showing you are 18 or older. Age is self-reported.

How long we keep things

We keep your data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account, except the report and deletion records described below. [CONFIRM: any shorter retention to promise, and how long backups and provider logs (Sentry, Resend, AI providers) keep data.]

Your controls

Circle: accept or decline requests, remove a friend, block, report, and switch visits and compare on or off per friend. Notifications: ATO asks once, after your first insight; change it in your phone's settings or on the You tab at any time. ATO's AI: turn it on or off on the You tab.

Delete your account: always available in-app, at the bottom of the You tab. It deletes your account and the data stored under it (profile, answers, Checks, saved profiles, Circle links, requests and switches, Divecore save, AI usage log and token history) and revokes your Sign in with Apple token. It is permanent. Two things are kept: a short deletion record (an ID, whether an Apple identity existed, the result of the Apple revoke; no profile content), and reports filed by or about you, without your account ID. Cards in the shared AI library stay with no link to you. [CONFIRM: retention of the deletion record, reports and backups.]
