What we collect

Account & profile — name, @handle, timezone (read from your device, not asked), city for Around (typed by you; we do not use GPS), date of birth (self-reported; we store the date so we can confirm you are 18 or older), onboarding answers and your answers to ATO's questions (which build your trait profile), your color and pixel appearance.

Usage — daily Checks (did/skip), your saved profiles and history, messages you send in Chat with people in your Circle, referral relationships if invite or referral links are in use (used only to prevent abuse, never shown publicly), your Divecore game save (your pet, rooms, items and progress; kept if you reset your profile, deleted when you delete your account), anonymous daily counts of Divecore actions (no account or device attached), and an AI usage log (which AI feature was used on which day and how many times, plus your ATO token activity; no request or reply text; kept if you reset your profile). [CONFIRM: email form on the public landing page — it currently collects an email to request an invite; keep, change or remove to match launch.]

Device — push notification token if enabled [CONFIRM: whether v1 uploads any token], crash and error reports through Sentry [CONFIRM: what Sentry receives and retention].

What we never collect or store: guesses about your mood or personality you didn't confirm, raw health data from your device, a model's freeform narrative written about you, your exact location.

What we do not do with what you type: we do not scan, flag or log it for safety or crisis detection, and nothing you type is sent to an AI model.

Who else sees your data

We use a small number of service providers to run ATO. We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication. Your profile, Checks and chat messages live here.
  • An AI model provider — used only for Legends cards and category deep dives. Everything else, including Insights, is written ahead of time; no model is called for it. The request goes from our server to the provider; your phone holds no provider key and sends only the kind of card and a trait bucket, never text you typed. The server builds the prompt and nothing personal (no name, no user ID, no answers) is included in shared cards; a deep dive uses your category leanings. Help if you're struggling

ATO is not a counselling or emergency service. On the You tab there is a fixed list of support lines: 988 for the US and Canada, findahelpline.com elsewhere, and your local emergency number. It is the same for everyone, it is not triggered by anything you type, and nothing about it is logged.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line directly.

Chat is not end-to-end encrypted

Messages in Chat are encrypted in transit (TLS) and protected by access controls in our database, but are not end-to-end encrypted. It is technically possible for us to access message content if required (for example, to investigate a report of abuse). We don't read your messages otherwise.

Referral tracking

If you were invited to ATO, we keep a record of who invited you, used only to prevent abuse. Never shown publicly beyond your own choice to disclose who you've invited.

Age requirement

ATO is for people 18 and older. Marking yourself "going" to an 18+ night requires you to be 18 or older. Age is self-reported.
