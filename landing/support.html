Support

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Email support@emgens.com. A real person reads it. Privacy requests are answered within 30 days.

Someone is bothering you

In your Circle, tap ⋯ next to the person, then Report (pick one of six reasons) or Block. In a chat, Report and Block are at the top of the screen, and you can long-press a message to report it. A block is silent. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.

Delete your account

Open the You tab, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. It is permanent. What is deleted and what is kept is in the Privacy Policy.

See or correct your data

Email us from the address you sign in with and say what you want to see or correct. We answer within 30 days.

Turn AI on or off

You tab → ATO's AI → On or Off.

Can't sign in

Sign-in codes come by email and can take a minute; check your spam folder. If you used Sign in with Apple, use it again on the same Apple account.

If you're struggling

ATO is not a crisis service and support email is not watched around the clock. In the US or Canada, call or text 988. Elsewhere, see findahelpline.com. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
