# first_cards.md — bank content, check_count < 3

Used before there's enough history for the model to personalize (Day 1–3 only).
`{morning_cue}` is substituted with the user's actual onboarding answer at render time.
Which style slot (quiet / even / loud) is chosen from energy_pattern + support_style
(see `bankStyleFor` in intake.ts), not from a fixed talk_style sequence.
Do is always if-then, anchored to `{morning_cue}`. No placeholder lorem — this is real copy.

---

## Day 1

### quiet
Read: First one logged. No pressure — just seeing what's actually true day to day.
Do: After you {morning_cue}, sit for one minute before opening your phone.

### even
Read: Day one. This is just information gathering — nothing to perform yet.
Do: After you {morning_cue}, write down one thing you're walking into today.

### loud
Read: Here we go — day one is just showing up once. That's the whole job today.
Do: Right after you {morning_cue}, say out loud what you're doing next. Just say it.

---

## Day 2

### quiet
Read: Two days in. Not a pattern yet, just a start that's still standing.
Do: After you {morning_cue}, do the smallest version of today's one big thing.

### even
Read: Second day. Consistency isn't about yesterday going well — it's about showing up again.
Do: After you {morning_cue}, pick the one task you'd regret skipping and do it first.

### loud
Read: Day two! You're back — that's the part most people skip. Keep the streak breathing.
Do: Right after you {morning_cue}, knock out the thing you've been putting off. Now.

---

## Day 3

### quiet
Read: Three days. Small and steady beats big and gone by Thursday.
Do: After you {morning_cue}, give yourself five real minutes on what matters most today.

### even
Read: Three in a row is enough to start noticing what actually fits your days.
Do: After you {morning_cue}, do one thing that moves your week forward, not just today.

### loud
Read: Three days straight — that's a real start, not beginner's luck. Don't stop here.
Do: Right after you {morning_cue}, go do the hardest small thing on your list first.
