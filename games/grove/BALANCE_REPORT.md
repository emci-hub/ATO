# Play balance report

**How to read a cell:** `✓` = a no-gear player clears it; `✗3` = 3 creeps leak at ×1 damage (the wave is lost). The number is the **damage needed** to clear with zero leaks: under 1× is comfortable, 1×–2.0× needs gear, over 2.0× is beyond the gear soft cap.
