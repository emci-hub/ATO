# Divecore notification-like surfaces: full inventory

Scope: `src/play/**` and `src/app/play.tsx`. Read-only audit, 2026-10-14. Layout claims are reasoned from style code, not measured on a device ("reasoned").
Word counts are for the words as written; `{x}` = template slot, `~n` = typical.

## Shorthands (to keep rows short)

- **PB** = PlayBanner queue (`play.tsx:1064`, `play-banner.tsx`). Renders: absolute overlay at `insets.top + 52`, left/right 12, zIndex 50, elevation 50, above every Play screen. Covers: the top ~70-106 pt of whatever is under it (see "PlayBanner facts"). Duration: 3200 ms after it reaches the front (+220 ms slide-out), so a queued one can wait up to 5 x ~3.4 s. Dismiss: tap (goes to `target`, or just dismisses when null), swipe up 24 px, or timeout. VoiceOver: one `accessibilityRole=button`, label `"{title}. {body}"`, hint "Opens it"/"Dismisses the notice"; no live region, no `announceForAccessibility`. Reduce Motion: no slide/fade, appears and vanishes instantly (swipe still moves it). Body is clamped to 3 lines.
- **SHEET** = `PlaySheet` (`play-sheet.tsx`): absolute full-screen layer inside the screen (not a native Modal), dimmed backdrop (tap = close), X button, drag-down to close, `accessibilityViewIsModal`, Reduce Motion = opacity fade instead of slide.
- **Class key**: NOTIFY async news, STATUS ongoing state (-> HUD), FEEDBACK result of own tap (-> on the control), ERROR refused action, CELEBRATE rare/hatch/milestone, HELP tips/coach, CONFIRM needs a decision.
- No `Alert.alert` and no React Native `<Modal>` exist anywhere under `src/play` or `play.tsx` (grep clean). "Modals" are all absolute-positioned Views.
