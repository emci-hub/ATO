# Almanac calendar, ramps and arcs

Generated by `npm run check:almanac-calendar -- --write` (ATO-BUILD PHASE-11b). Do not edit by hand: the check fails if this file is stale.

## How a day gets its theme

- Every day has a **season**, a **month theme** and a **weekly trait theme**, so Home is never empty.
- On top of that an **event** (or a one-day **micro-event**) may be on. Events are laid out as `teaser | lead | build | PEAK | fade`, with durations in days counted back from the peak's first day.
- Target intensity: teaser 0.10–0.20 · lead 0.20–0.40 · build 0.40–0.80 · peak 1.0 · fade 1 → 0, smoothstep inside each band. Ambient events stop at 0.4.
- **Content** (which cards may show) follows the winner: personal day (its lead and fade rank like any other approach) > regional peak > global peak > lead/fade/build/teaser > ambient > micro; ties go to the higher target, then the id.
- **Visuals** follow one lane with one owner. Its intensity follows the owner's target through a slew limit of 0.3 a day. The owner changes only once the lane is at 0.3 or lower, so a losing event slews down instead of snapping, and at most one event is ever shown above 0.4.
- State is derived, never stored: the lane for a day is rebuilt by walking back 30 days.
- Card blend: winner target ≥ 0.9 → line + deck seasonal; ≥ 0.4 → try slot seasonal; teaser phase → one teaser card; otherwise the week theme sets the try slot and the month theme sets the line 1 day in 3. Afterglow card on the first fade day; callbacks are keyed `eventId:year`.
- Master switch off: events and micro-events go away; seasons, months and weeks stay. Birthday and ATO anniversary stay (they are personal, not seasonal).

## Micro-events

One day, intensity 0.4 at most. On a shared day the first in this list wins. `small_moment` fills quiet days: every 10th day since the Pick epoch with nothing else on, so no stretch is empty for more than 9 days.

| Id | Name | When |
|---|---|---|
| `pick_100` | Your 100th Pick | the day the reader answers their 100th Pick (needs `pickCount` from the app) |
| `glow_30` | 30-day glow | the day the glow streak reaches 30 (`streakWithRest`) |
| `one_year_ago` | One year ago today | a Pick was answered on this date last year (`answeredYearAgo`) |
| `equinox` | Equinox | March and September equinox, local day |
| `full_moon` | Full moon | full-moon table, local day |
| `friday_13th` | Friday the 13th | any Friday the 13th |
| `clock_change` | Clock change | the local day the zone's UTC offset changes |
| `half_year` | Half-year | Jul 1 |
| `first_snow` | First snow | north Nov 20 · south Jul 20 · off in the tropics |
| `payday_friday` | Payday Friday | the last Friday of the month |
| `small_moment` | A small moment | quiet-day filler |

## Sources

- Lunar New Year: chinahighlights.com, vclock.com, chinesenewyear.net (agree).
- Diwali (Lakshmi Puja): Wikipedia, drikpanchang.com, testbook.com. Where sources differ by a day (2031, 2034) the earlier date is stored and Diwali has a 2-day peak.
- Eid al-Fitr: Wikipedia, truecalendar.com (Umm al-Qura). Moon sighting moves it by a day by country, so Eid has a 2-day peak that starts on the earliest listed date. 2033 has two.
- Equinoxes, solstices, full moons: Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms ch. 27 and 49, UTC to the minute; 2026 values match the published times.
- Mother's Day: US/CA/AU/NZ 2nd Sunday of May; UK/IE Mothering Sunday = Easter − 21. Father's Day: AU/NZ 1st Sunday of September, other known regions 3rd Sunday of June. A region with no rule gets no event.
