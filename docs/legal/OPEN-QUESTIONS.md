# Open questions (2026-10-20)

1. **AI vendor.** Which vendor(s) does `ai-library` / `ai-library-fill` call in production? The code (`supabase/functions/_shared/ai-vendors.ts`) can call Google Gemini (default model `gemini-3.7-flash`) and DeepSeek (`deepseek-v4-flash`, apparently a fallback), chosen by Supabase secrets. `docs/NOW.md` says Gemini with DeepSeek as the fallback. Name only the live one(s) in the policy and terms. (`scripts/floor-check.ts` currently requires the word "Gemini" in both `privacy.md` and the labels; if DeepSeek-only or another vendor, that check must change.)
2. **Vendor terms.** For each live AI vendor: do its API terms bar training on requests, and how long does it retain them? The policy claims "we don't use your content to train models" and that providers may not use data for their own purposes. That is only true if the paid/API terms say so. Is the Gemini key on a paid tier (free tiers often allow training)? Is it OK that DeepSeek is a China-based processor if it is live? (Lawyer.)
3. **Retention periods.** None are stated in the repo. Needed for: chat messages, Checks, answers and saved profiles (kept forever per `CLAUDE.md`), the AI usage log (`ai_usage`, `ai_provider_log`), token events, Sentry reports, Resend email logs, Supabase backups, the `account_deletions` audit row, anonymous Divecore action counts, `access_requests` emails.
4. **What deleting an account removes.** `delete-account` deletes the auth user and relies on every foreign key being `ON DELETE CASCADE`; it then counts rows still referencing the user (`count_user_rows`) and writes an `account_deletions` audit row (user id, Apple identity yes/no, Apple revoke status; no profile content) that deliberately survives. Please confirm: (a) the audit row is acceptable and for how long it is kept; (b) backups and provider logs; (c) rows about you that live in other people's data (messages you sent in their threads, reports about you, Circle links) are removed or kept; (d) old `sage_messages`, `crisis_flags` and `me.facts` rows are covered by the cascade (wave103 is the drop for crisis plumbing and is unapplied).
5. **"Reset account".** It is a root-only, pre-launch tool (`reset_my_account` needs root and invite-only mode), so the legal text does not mention it. If you ever offer a reset to public users, the text must say what it keeps (consent, Circle, AI usage log, Divecore save).
6. **Governing law and dispute resolution.** Alberta, Canada was in the earlier draft. Arbitration clause or courts? Is the exclusion of consequential damages enforceable for consumers in your target countries? Do Apple's required terms (App Store EULA) need to be referenced? (Lawyer.)
7. **Company / legal entity name.** Every document says "AsTrollOGs". Is that the registered entity? Also confirm the contact domain spelling `support@asstrollogs.com` and that the inbox is monitored (`docs/NOW.md` says not yet confirmed). Address or other required contact info for privacy law (PIPEDA / Alberta PIPA, GDPR / UK if you accept users there)?
8. **Privacy-law scope at public launch.** Which countries can sign up? Do you need a GDPR / UK representative, a CCPA notice, a lawful-basis statement, a data-subject-request process (access, export, correction) beyond in-app delete, or a transfer statement for Supabase / vendors hosted abroad? (Lawyer.)
9. **Age-rating answers (App Store Connect).** 18+ only, user-to-user chat, AI-generated content, "going" to 18+ nights, mentions of death/drinking/money and strong swearing / adult humor under the core content policy. Pick the questionnaire answers (frequent/infrequent mature themes, user-generated content, AI) and confirm the resulting rating; Apple also expects moderation (report/block) and a contact point for user-generated content. Not answerable from the repo.
