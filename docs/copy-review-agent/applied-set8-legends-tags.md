# Set 8 — Legends: trait tag re-audit (2026-10-19)

emci (2026-10-19): "Remove unsupported historical trait inferences — don't fill quotas with guesses." Every one of the 518 tags on the 120 legends was re-judged. A tag stays only when the behaviour it names is directly documented: the person's own words, or a reliable source plainly describing that behaviour (for myths and animals: plainly in the tale or the documented story). Tags inferred from a role, a circumstance, an outcome or status, one event, or something done TO the person were removed, as were tags whose behaviour is real but does not show the trait named (for example "sensitive playing" as emotional sensitivity). No replacement guesses were added.

**Result:** 289 kept (12 with a rewritten, sourced why), 229 removed, 3 added. 292 tags remain (was 518). 20 legends now carry a single tag.

The 3 added tags are not quota fills. Two move a documented behaviour to the axis it names (Michelangelo: "by nature a solitary" person, now Reserved instead of the attachment label; Duke Kahanamoku: "trained and loaned equipment to new surfers", now Giving instead of Easygoing). Wilma Rudolph lost all four tags (status, outcomes, polio done to her), so she gets one documented one (she refused to attend her segregated homecoming, and it became Clarksville's first integrated event), because every legend needs at least one tag.

Sources key: **WP** = the legend's English Wikipedia page. The new or rewritten whys were checked against the live page on 2026-10-19; the phrase is quoted. Other KEEP rows rest on the WP phrases already re-verified in `applied-set8-legends.md`.

Explicit re-judgements asked for: Korematsu extraversion low REMOVE, Henson autonomy low REMOVE, all 4 added attachment_avoidance tags REMOVE (Tesla, Michelangelo, Dickinson, Bashō). Of the 6 kept attachment tags: Earhart KEEP (her own wedding-day letter), Van Gogh KEEP with a new why (he confided in Theo in 600+ letters); Sojourner Truth, Ida B. Wells, Blankers-Koen and Tenzing REMOVE. Every other coverage add on a real person was removed except the ones resting on the person's own words or a plain source description: Hepburn, Lamarr, Tagore (own words), Astaire, Nightingale, Ruth (agreeableness low) KEEP; Bach, Fleming, Barton, Ryōkan (assertiveness low) REMOVE. Every interpretive myth or animal tag added for coverage was removed (Robin Hood, King Arthur, Beowulf, Mulan, Scheherazade, Nasreddin, Flying Dutchman, Brownie, Headless Horseman, Loch Ness, Yeti, Will-o'-the-wisp competence, Leprechaun, Hachikō, Togo, Seabiscuit, Clever Hans, Balto, Greyfriars Bobby) except Momotarō conscientiousness low (lazy-boy versions are documented, hedged), Banshee locus low (plainly what she does), Tsukumogami attachment_anxiety high (plainly in the scrolls, hedged) and Owney attachment_anxiety low (documented).

## Gate change (`scripts/legend-roster-check.ts`)

- **Per legend:** 3–5 tags → 1–5 tags. Every legend still has at least one tag, so "Where you match" (or its contrast pair) is never empty.
- **Trait sides:** the hard "every side ≥ 8 visible legends" quota is replaced by a printed warning for sides under the target of 8, plus a pinned list `TRAIT_SIDES_EMPTY` of the sides no visible legend carries. The gate fails if a new side empties (a tag lost without a decision) or a pinned side gains a tag (update the list). Coverage cannot drift silently.
- **Halls:** "≥ 15 sides and ≥ 4 low sides per hall" is replaced by a printed per-hall summary. Still asserted: ≥ 10 legends per hall, and ≥ 3 open (non-hidden) legends, so no hall starts with an empty frame.
- **Matching:** for every side the roster carries, a strong lean still finds a legend with that side as the best match AND behind some hall's frames (unchanged guarantee, now 29 sides). New: for every side, carried or not, all three frames in all 12 halls are filled and every framed legend yields a story pair.

Why this is not a weakening: the old quota guaranteed numbers, not truth, and could only be met by inventing tags. The guarantees a reader sees still hold and are tested: no empty frame, no empty card, a carried side is always reachable, and the order still weights matches first. What is no longer guaranteed (a match on every side) was never honestly true; the three empty sides are listed in the gate and here.

Hall/frames code: unchanged. `hallOrder` gives every open legend a weight floor (`LEGEND_WEIGHT_FLOOR`), so frames fill from any legend in the hall whatever the tags; `legendPairs` falls back to the first tag as a contrast pair. Both now pinned by the new test.
