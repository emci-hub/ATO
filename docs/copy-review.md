# Copy sheet — read before publishing

Everything below is static text in the app that is now marked as reviewed. AI-written
text (insight, category cards, Story, AI questions, AI daily lines) is not here: it is
written fresh each time and stays marked as draft.

Tell me any line to change and I fix it before publishing. The 340 daily lines are in
docs/daily-line-review.md.

## 1. Category lines on Explore (new voice)

What a category row says before its AI card is loaded, and when AI is off.
Three versions each: leaning low, in the middle, leaning high.

**Steadiness**
- Low: Plans stay loose with you, and one small knock can stay for the afternoon.
- Middle: Some days you follow through, some days you wobble. It depends which day you ask.
- High: You finish the plan and forget the bad morning by lunch.

**Openness to life**
- Low: You like the known route and the quieter room, and you would pick both again.
- Middle: Mostly the familiar, with the occasional detour when someone talks you into it.
- High: You said yes to the new thing, and it helped that people were going.

**Drive**
- Low: A plan someone else made is fine by you, and a hard task gets a long look first.
- Middle: Some days you pick the path. Some days you take the one already there.
- High: You want it done your way, and you figure you can handle the hard part.

**Agency**
- Low: When it falls apart, you tend to figure it was going to, and that road can feel closed.
- Middle: Sometimes you look for what to change. Sometimes you let it be.
- High: After a miss you go looking for what to change, and the bigger ask still looks doable.

**Everyday social energy**
- Low: Quiet is how you reset, and the jokes can wait until you have had some.
- Middle: People and jokes come out when the day has room for them.
- High: You make the plans, fill the room, and keep it light while you do.

**Communication**
- Low: In a disagreement you go quiet and hold on to what you came for.
- Middle: Sometimes you say your piece. Sometimes you leave the room for theirs.
- High: You say your piece out loud and still look for the version they can live with.

**Love / closeness**
- Low: A slow reply is just a slow reply to you, and once in, you stay close.
- Middle: You want a real check-in and still keep a little room for yourself.
- High: A pause can feel like someone pulling away, and a text feels safer than a call.

**Independence & closeness**
- Low: A plan someone else made suits you, and a day can go fine without much contact.
- Middle: Your own way some days, a real check-in on others.
- High: You want to pick the path, and you still need someone real in the day for it to count.

**Levity**
- Low: A hard talk is a job to you. The lightness can wait until it is done.
- Middle: Sometimes you leave a little air in a hard talk. Sometimes you do not.
- High: Even your hard talks have a bit of air in them.

**Structure vs. spontaneity**
- Low: The plan drifts once the day gets boring, and something new does not pull that hard either.
- Middle: Some days the plan holds. Some days a new idea wins.
- High: You finish the plan even when it drags, and still keep a tab open for what is next.

**Resilience under pressure**
- Low: A hard task can make you doubt yourself, and a miss can feel like the end of that road.
- Middle: Some days the hard thing looks doable. A knock lingers, then fades.
- High: Hard things look handleable to you, a miss is a note for next time, and a knock is gone by lunch.

For three categories, a more exact version is used once both of their traits are settled:

**Love / closeness**
- A slow reply is just a slow reply to you, and once in, you stay close.
- A pause can feel like someone pulling away, and you move closer anyway.
- A slow reply does not bother you, and you like a little distance of your own.
- A pause can feel like someone pulling away, and you keep some distance just in case.

**Independence & closeness**
- A plan someone else made suits you, and a day can go fine without much contact.
- You pick your own path, and a day can go fine without much contact.
- A plan someone else made suits you, as long as your people are in it.
- You want to pick the path, and you still need someone real in the day for it to count.

**Structure vs. spontaneity**
- You like a set path, and it still drifts once the day gets boring.
- You trade the plan for something new quickly, and let the new thing drift just as fast.
- You like a set path and you stay on it, even on the boring days.
- You chase the new thing and still finish the plan you already made.

## 3. Traits: names, the word for each end, and what it sounds like (approved 2026-10-02)

Every trait is one noun. Each end has its own word, plus one thing a person at that end
would say. The app shows yours as, for example: You lean Adventurous: “What’s that? I’ll try it.”
Tapping the **?** beside the trait name opens what the trait means, the opposite end
(by its word, with its quote), and which categories the trait is part of.

|---|---|---|---|

| **Follow-through** | Flexible: “I’ll figure it out when I get there.” | Structured: “I said I’d finish it, so I did.” | Steadiness, Structure vs. spontaneity |

The same names, words and quotes are used in the two other explanations (both closed until tapped):

The trait names also head each question on the Questions screen and the "Traits answered" list.

## 4. Unlock and after-50 wording (unchanged, factual)

- Open now: Your daily insight, on Home · Categories, on Explore · Your next 25 questions, here
- What your next 25 are for: A third answer on each of your 16 traits, which is what a trait needs to settle · Your Story on Home, which needs more settled traits first · More categories on Explore · +21 ATO tokens
- Not open yet: Sage and Legends are being rebuilt. Nothing you do here unlocks them yet.
- +21 ATO tokens, in your balance on You
- A trait settles after three answers that agree. The 50 give most traits two, so they read as still settling until your next 25.
- Round complete
- After a round: "N of 16 settled. Your next 25 keep settling the rest." or, once everything is settled, "N of 16 settled. Every round from here sharpens your reads."
- Locked line: "Locked — finish all 50 questions in the Questions tab to unlock this (N of 50 done)."

The "Traits answered" checklist on Explore (unchanged):
- Traits answered
- One answer is enough to fill a trait in. Filling one is not the same as settling it — settling takes a few answers that agree.
- all answered once
- Every trait has at least one answer. Questions can go anywhere from here.
- Filled

## 5. Notifications

- Morning: title "ATO", body is today's daily line. If the line is private: "Today’s line is ready."
- Sunday: title "This week", body "You showed up 4." (the number is the days you opened the app that week; nothing is sent for a week with none)
- Evening: switched off. It asked people to log a Check, which no longer exists.
- Category push (Mon/Fri): still off. It would put AI-written text on the lock screen.

## 6. The mini guy

Idle lines:
- Hi. I live up here.
- Nothing new. You checked anyway.
- You tapped me to see what would happen. This is what happens.
- All caught up. Suspicious.
- No news. I would have said.
- Still here. Still small.
- No notifications. Enjoy the quiet.
- That tickles, for the record.
- Tap again, same guy.
- I was mid-nap, but go on.
- Yes, I felt that.
- Nothing to report. I checked twice.

Milestones he announces:
- Halfway through the 50: 25 answered. The other 25 open your insight, categories and your next rounds.
- 12 answers in: Twelve down. Past the small-talk stage.
- 24 answers in: Twenty-four in. This is officially a habit.
- 36 answers in: Thirty-six. The end of the 50 is in sight.
- Halfway there: Half of your traits have settled. The other half is still deciding.
- 3-day streak: Three days in a row. That counts as a pattern now.
- 7-day streak: Seven days straight. A whole week of showing up.
- 21-day streak: Twenty-one days. This is just part of the day now.
