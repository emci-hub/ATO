Aura colour picker

Pick an element, a style, then a colour. Single colour: one colour carries the whole effect. Two colours: a main colour plus a different second colour for the smaller pieces (fire: flames + embers, water: drips and bubbles + orbiting bubbles, earth: petals + rocks, thunder: bolts + arcs). The colour is in the effect itself, not an outline around the pet. Shown at 2x.
