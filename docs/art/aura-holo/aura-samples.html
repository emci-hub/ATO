Your calls

1 · Aura look
  1. Bold, as above (recommended): big silhouette shapes in Full mode, a slow 2-frame breathing version for Reduce Motion.
  2. Quieter: floor ring + a few motes only, so the pet always wins the eye.
2 · Element colour
  1. Keep one sheet per style, recolour on the phone (recommended): art drawn in 4 grey steps, each element maps them to 4 colours taken from the night palette. Any element or custom colour still works, files stay small, and pixels stay exact.
  2. Bake every element separately: 4 styles × 6 elements = 24 sheets. Hand-tuned colour, about 6× the file size.
3 · Holo
  1. C for holo, B for reverse-holo (recommended): two finishes you can tell apart at a glance.
  2. B for both.
  3. C for both.

Research behind the picks

  • Aura on its own layer behind the sprite, looping 6–8 frames: Paint.NET forum, Aseprite community, top-down aura pack (96 px frames).
  • Outline glow costs a rebuild per frame; better baked or shader-driven: MonoGame forum.
  • Palette/colour cycling: still pixels, moving colour bands; cheap and very pixel-native: Colour cycling, Mark Ferrari's work, 80.lv.
  • Foil reads strongest on light colours, barely on dark; keep dark outlines so icons don't vanish: The Game Crafter.
  • Keep foil off the subject or mask it, or hotspots burn through: ShaderKit docs, Pokémon card masks.
  • Pixel-grid foil with stepped bands (Balatro-style): Balatro foil port, 2D holographic card shader.
