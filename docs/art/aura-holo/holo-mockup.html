The glint, frame by frame

Seven frames at 12 fps: quick grow, a held peak with diagonal sparks, slow fade. White core, the chosen colour on the arms, the darker step on the tips so it reads on light and dark ground.

Colour mixes

Two kinds. Single colour: one colour dark → light → dark, so the bands shimmer instead of flipping; Opal, Chrome and Gold are neutral metals and the rest match the game's elements. Mixes: several colours played in order and back, for the loud rainbow-foil look.

Glints: White by default (a glint reflects the light, not the foil); Match band follows the prism colour.

Research
